CONVERSATIONAL AI · CX

Vertex AI Conversational Assistant

The challenge

Customers can engage with AI yet still transfer to human agents, increasing cost per conversation and signalling unresolved intent.

Product approach

Diagnose the funnel from AI entry to resolution, segment transfer reasons and validate whether friction comes from comprehension, capability or trust.

Experience direction

Intent understanding · guided responses · clear next steps · escalation with context

Success measures / outcome

AI resolution rate · human transfer rate · completion rate · cost per conversation · CSAT

What this demonstrates

Customer problem framing, product judgement, cross-functional thinking, prioritisation and a metrics-led approach.
