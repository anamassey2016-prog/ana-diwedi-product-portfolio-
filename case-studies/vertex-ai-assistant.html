GENAI · CONVERSATIONAL PRODUCT

Vertex AI Conversational Assistant

A product analytics framework for improving AI-assisted journeys when customers engage with the assistant but still transfer to human agents.

THE CHALLENGE

Engagement is not the same as resolution.

High conversation volume can look positive while completion remains weak and service costs rise. The real question is where customers abandon the automated journey, why they transfer, and which intents AI can resolve reliably.

MY PRODUCT APPROACH

How I would move from problem to outcome.

01

Instrument

Track entry, intent, response, action, completion and transfer events.

02

Segment

Break performance down by intent, cohort, journey stage and failure reason.

03

Diagnose

Combine funnel data with conversation review to identify comprehension or capability gaps.

04

Improve

Prioritize fixes and validate whether resolution improves without harming experience.

SUCCESS

What I would measure.

Adoption · intent recognition · task completion · containment · transfer rate · drop-off by step · cost per resolution · CSAT · repeat usage

PRODUCT TAKEAWAY

Optimize for resolved intent, not conversation volume.

The framework separates adoption, engagement and successful completion so that product decisions are tied to actual customer outcomes.
