TELECOM · E-COMMERCE

BYOD Commerce Journey

Simplifying device validation, compatibility and next-step decisions in a Bring Your Own Device acquisition funnel.

THE CHALLENGE

Uncertainty compounds at every step of a device-eligibility journey.

Customers need confidence that their device is eligible, compatible and ready for the selected offer. Ambiguous status or weak calls to action can increase abandonment before checkout.

MY PRODUCT APPROACH

How I would move from problem to outcome.

01

Map

Instrument validation through checkout and locate the largest drop-offs.

02

Clarify

Make eligibility and compatibility status immediately understandable.

03

Guide

Create a clear validation → review → offer → checkout progression.

04

Measure

Compare step conversion, errors, time to complete and final conversion.

SUCCESS

What I would measure.

Validation completion · validation failure · step conversion · abandonment · checkout conversion · time to complete

PRODUCT TAKEAWAY

Confidence is a conversion feature.

This case focuses on mobile-first clarity, decision support and funnel measurement rather than adding unnecessary steps or features.
