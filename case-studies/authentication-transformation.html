BANKING · SECURITY · UX

Authentication & Approval Transformation

A product approach to reducing authentication friction in high-value banking while preserving fraud controls, compliance and auditability.

THE CHALLENGE

Security friction becomes a product problem when legitimate customers cannot complete critical tasks.

OTP-heavy approval journeys can create delays, failures, abandonment and support contacts. The challenge is to improve legitimate-user success without increasing fraud exposure or weakening controls.

MY PRODUCT APPROACH

How I would move from problem to outcome.

01

Risk-map

Segment journeys by transaction value, risk, role and approval model.

02

Diagnose

Measure OTP failures, delays, abandonment and false-positive friction.

03

Design

Evaluate adaptive authentication, behavioral/device signals and resilient fallbacks.

04

Balance

Review customer, fraud, compliance and operational metrics together.

SUCCESS

What I would measure.

Transaction success · authentication failure · approval time · abandonment · fraud rate · false positives · support contacts · NPS

PRODUCT TAKEAWAY

Security and experience should be optimized as one system.

Risk ecosystems may incorporate fraud and behavioral analytics capabilities such as Falcon and BioCatch alongside transaction, identity and device signals. Specific employer architecture is intentionally not disclosed.
