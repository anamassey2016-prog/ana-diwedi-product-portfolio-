Authentication & Approval Transformation

The challenge

OTP-heavy authentication can create delayed approvals, transaction drop-offs and support friction.

Product approach

Reduce authentication friction without weakening security, auditability, regulatory compliance or fraud controls.

Experience direction

Risk-aware authentication · better approval visibility · behavioural signals · reduced avoidable friction

Success measures / outcome

Transaction success · approval completion · authentication failure · support contacts · fraud guardrails

What this demonstrates

Customer problem framing, product judgement, cross-functional thinking, prioritisation and a metrics-led approach.
