AI / GENAI · AGENTIC COMMERCE

AI Agentic Commerce

Reimagining commerce as an intelligent conversation that understands intent, reduces decision effort and helps customers progress from discovery to transaction.

THE CHALLENGE

Commerce journeys often force customers to do the orchestration.

Customers navigate pages, compare products, interpret complex information and decide what to do next. The opportunity is not simply to add a chatbot, but to let AI remove cognitive work while deterministic systems retain control of critical actions.

MY PRODUCT APPROACH

How I would move from problem to outcome.

01

Discover

Map high-friction decisions and customer jobs.

02

Define

Separate agent responsibilities from deterministic workflows and human support.

03

Design

Create an intent-led journey from discovery to recommendation to action.

04

Validate

Test usefulness, trust, guardrails and conversion behavior.

SUCCESS

What I would measure.

AI adoption · recommendation engagement · journey completion · conversion · human-agent transfer · cost per resolved conversation · CSAT · repeat AI usage

PRODUCT TAKEAWAY

AI should reduce customer effort, not add another interface.

This concept demonstrates how I frame AI as a product capability tied to customer and business outcomes, with explicit guardrails and escalation paths.
