AI Agentic Commerce

The challenge

How can an AI agent understand customer intent, reduce decision overload and help complete a commerce journey rather than simply return search results?

Product approach

Frame the opportunity around customer goals, decision friction and measurable journey completion.

Experience direction

Intent capture · contextual recommendations · guided decisioning · transparent actions · human control

Success measures / outcome

Adoption · journey completion · assisted conversion · handoff rate · customer satisfaction

What this demonstrates

Customer problem framing, product judgement, cross-functional thinking, prioritisation and a metrics-led approach.
