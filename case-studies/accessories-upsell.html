Accessories Recommendation & Upsell

The challenge

Accessory recommendations lose trust when products shown are incompatible or poorly timed.

Product approach

Prioritise compatibility first, then relevance and contextual placement to make cross-sell useful.

Experience direction

Compatibility filtering · contextual recommendations · relevant bundles

Success measures / outcome

Attach rate · recommendation CTR · conversion · revenue per order

What this demonstrates

Customer problem framing, product judgement, cross-functional thinking, prioritisation and a metrics-led approach.
