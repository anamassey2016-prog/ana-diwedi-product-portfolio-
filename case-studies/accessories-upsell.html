Accessories Recommendation & Upsell

Turning a potentially frustrating cross-sell moment into a compatibility-aware recommendation experience.

THE CHALLENGE

Irrelevant recommendations damage both conversion and trust.

When accessories shown during a device journey are incompatible or poorly matched, customers lose confidence and the business loses a high-intent revenue opportunity.

MY PRODUCT APPROACH

How I would move from problem to outcome.

01

Context

Use selected-device and journey context as the foundation for recommendations.

02

Filter

Remove incompatible products before ranking or merchandising.

03

Recommend

Surface a small set of relevant accessories at the right moment.

04

Optimize

Measure attach, conversion and order value while monitoring bad recommendations.

SUCCESS

What I would measure.

Accessory attach rate · recommendation CTR · add-to-cart · conversion · average order value · incompatible-item exposure

PRODUCT TAKEAWAY

Relevance comes before upsell.

The goal is not to maximize the number of recommendations shown; it is to make each recommendation credible enough to help the customer and create incremental value.
